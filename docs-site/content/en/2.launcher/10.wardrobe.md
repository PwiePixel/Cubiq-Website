**Skins** in the sidebar is your wardrobe: collect skins and try them on in a 3D preview, pick your Mojang cape and dress up with Cubiq capes, wings and companions that everyone with the Cubiq Client can see. The page has three tabs: **Skins**, **Capes** and **Cosmetics**.

![The Skins page with the 3D preview and your skin collection](/shots/launcher/skins.png)

## Change your skin

::steps{level="3"}
### Try it on
Click a skin under **Saved skins** to see it on the 3D figure. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and switch between **Walk**, **Idle** and **Stand**.

### Pick model and cape
Choose the **Model** – **Classic** (4-pixel arms, Steve) or **Slim** (3-pixel arms, Alex). Your **Mojang capes** are in the **Capes** tab; **None** wears no cape.

### Apply
Nothing changes on your account until you click **Apply**. **Discard** throws your changes away.
::

The launcher sends the change to Mojang in the background. If Mojang slows down requests, it waits and applies the change by itself – you see a short note.

## Add skins

**Add skin** opens a menu:

- **Choose files** – one or more PNG files (64×64, or 64×32 for old skins). You can also drag PNG files into the window.
- **By link** – a public https link to a PNG image.
- **By player name** – copies the skin a player is wearing right now.
- **From other launchers** – skins you saved in the official Minecraft Launcher, Prism Launcher, the Modrinth App or ATLauncher.

The launcher recognises slim arms by itself; for a single skin you can still change the name and model. **Save current skin** adds the skin your account is wearing to your collection. You can rename and delete skins in your collection at any time.

::tip
With the Cubiq services and **Sync with Cubiq account** on, your saved skins are the same on all your PCs – and in the Cubiq Client's wardrobe in the game.
::

## Cubiq capes

Cubiq capes are separate from your Mojang capes: everyone who plays with the Cubiq Client sees them (some are animated). You find them in the **Capes** tab. They need the optional Cubiq services.

- Click a cape to try it on, then **Put on** – or **Take off**.
- Locked capes can be tried on anyway; some can be unlocked with a code, others come from [achievements](/en/launcher/achievements).
- Wings and companions are in the **Cosmetics** tab – see [Cosmetics](/en/launcher/wardrobe#cosmetics) below.

### Redeem a code

Click **Redeem code** at the top of the page or in the Cubiq capes section and enter the code (20 characters, for example `7K3QF-M2XPA-9RTVB-C4HJN` – case and hyphens don't matter). The unlocked cape or cosmetic appears in your collection right away.

### Upload your own cape

::steps{level="3"}
### Choose images
**Upload your own cape** → **Choose files**: any PNG, JPEG or WebP image, an animated GIF or APNG, several images as frames, a finished cape texture or sprite sheet, or a Cubiq Studio export. With **Choose video** you take a short section of a video instead.

### Pick the part
Drag and zoom to choose the part that goes on the cape and watch it live on the player. For animations, choose the speed. In the desktop launcher you can touch up every frame in a pixel editor before uploading.

### Upload
Up to 1024×512 pixels per frame, up to 32 frames, at most 20 MB. Click **Upload**.
::

The team reviews every cape – until then only you can see it. Only upload your own images or ones you're allowed to use.

### Share a cape with friends

Once your own cape is approved, **Share with a friend** offers it to a friend, who can accept or decline. Friends may pass it on to their friends – up to 20 players per cape. **Shared** shows who has it, and you can take it back at any time; everyone they passed it on to loses it too.

## Cosmetics

The **Cosmetics** tab holds 3D cosmetics that everyone with the Cubiq Client sees on you in the game. Like Cubiq capes, they need the optional Cubiq services (see [Privacy](/en/launcher/settings#privacy)). Each place on your character has its own section:

- **Wings** – sit on your back.
- **Companions** – fly around your head.
- **Ground companions** – walk along next to you.

You can wear one from each section at the same time – for example the aurora wings, the shooting star and the penguin together.

### Nine free cosmetics

Nine animated cosmetics are free for everyone: nothing to unlock, their cards say **Free** – just pick them. The cards show the German names of the cosmetics – the second column tells you which is which.

Some cosmetics are locked and show a small lock. You can still try them on; the box below the cards tells you how to get them – for example with a code (**Redeem code**) or an achievement (**Go to achievements**).

### Try on and put on

1. **Try it on** – click a card. The cosmetic appears on the 3D figure in the preview; a ground companion stands right next to it. Its name shows up at the top right of the preview – click it to end the preview. Only you see what you try on.
2. **Put it on** – click **Put on** in the box below the cards. From now on, everyone with the Cubiq Client sees it on you in the game.
3. **Take it off** – click the card of the cosmetic you wear and then **Take off**. **End preview** only stops trying on.

With the Cubiq Client you can also pick them in the game – see [Companions and cosmetics](/en/client/companions).

### Day, night and moves

The buttons around the preview show your cosmetics the way they look in the game:

- **Night** – the moon button below the preview turns day into night. Cosmetics with the **Glows** badge light up in the dark: Cubi's eyes and leaves, the axolotl's gills, the snowman's eyes, the firefly's lantern, the aurora wings, the shooting star and Mini-Cubi's face and sprout. The cards switch to their night picture too.
- **Zoom to head** – the button with the frame zooms in on the head, handy for the companions that fly around it (when you try one on, the preview zooms in by itself). Click the button again – **Show whole body** – to see the whole figure. **Reset camera** undoes your own turning and zooming but stays on the head or the whole figure, whichever is shown.
- The row **Wings:** with **Standing**, **In the air** and **Sneaking** shows the wings on the ground, in flight and while you sneak.
- The row **Companion:** with **Stands**, **Walks**, **Slides**, **Swims**, **Jumps**, **Sneaks** and **Hands up** shows how your ground companion moves in the game. The fox kit also **Grabs loot** and **Flees**.
- Some cosmetics have parts you can switch on and off, like the penguin's **Scarf** or the snowman's **Hat** and **Scarf**. The switches appear below the preview. For a cosmetic you wear, the change is saved right away and others see it in the game; otherwise it applies when you put it on.

![Aurora wings, shooting star and Mini-Cubi – by day at the top and at night at the bottom](/shots/launcher/cosmetics-day-night.png)

### Pet and feed in the preview

When a ground companion is in the preview, two small buttons sit in its bottom left corner: **Pet** with a heart and **Feed** with an apple. On phones you only see the two icons.

- **Pet** – your companion leans into your hand, closes its eyes happily, wiggles, and little hearts rise above it.
- **Feed** – it looks up, takes three bites with crumbs flying and chews happily.

Each reaction plays to the end, then your companion goes back to what it was doing. Point at a button to see how it works in the game: right-click your own companion with an empty hand to pet it, or while holding food to feed it – more in [Companions and cosmetics](/en/client/companions#pet-and-feed-your-companion). The [cosmetics page](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/cosmetics) on the website has the same two buttons.

![The Pet and Feed buttons in the bottom left corner of the companion preview, with the companion's moves below](/shots/launcher/cosmetics-pet-buttons.png)

## Emotes

Emotes and the emote wheel live in the Cubiq Client's wardrobe in the game. Rewards like the **Party** emote show up there automatically.
