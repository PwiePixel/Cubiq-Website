Cubiq Launcher keeps itself up to date. New versions are signed, download in the background while you play and install when you restart.

## Update the launcher

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="Windows / AppImage" icon="i-lucide-monitor"}
1. When a new version is out, the launcher downloads it in the background. The tasks panel shows "Downloading update in the background".
2. As soon as it's ready, a **Restart to update** button appears in the title bar.
3. Click it. You see a short "Updating – restarting in a moment …" screen, then the new version starts. Running games keep running.

If an update fails, the title bar shows why and offers **Retry update** or **Download installer**.
:::

:::tabs-item{label="Linux packages" icon="i-lucide-terminal"}
The .deb, .rpm and AUR packages are updated by your package manager. The launcher only tells you: "Update … available" with a link to the release.

- Arch (AUR): `yay -Syu` or `paru -Syu`
- Debian/Ubuntu: download the new `.deb` and install it with `sudo apt install ./Cubiq-Launcher_<version>_amd64.deb`
- Fedora/openSUSE: install the new `.rpm` with `dnf` or `zypper`
:::
::

## The Cubiq Client updates itself

The Cubiq Client has its own signed update channel and gets fixes without a new launcher version. It updates itself on the next game start. The current Cubiq Client version is shown at the bottom of the settings.

## What's new

After an update the launcher shows **What's new in …** once, with everything the new version brings:

- the **update banner** with the name of the update and its own small picture,
- a **gallery** with screenshots of the new features – click a picture to enlarge it, browse with the arrow keys,
- the **notes** of the update, written for players,
- **View release on GitHub** and **Got it** to close it.

Did you skip a few updates? Then "What's new" collects all of them: the version buttons at the bottom left (for example **v0.3.3**, **v0.3.2**, **v0.3.1**) switch between the updates, so you don't miss anything.

![What's new after several updates: banner, screenshot gallery and the version buttons at the bottom](/shots/launcher/update-news.webp)

::note
The notes are written in English and German. If the launcher is set to another language, you see the English notes with a short hint.
::

### An animated picture for every update

Every update has its own small animated pixel-art scene in its banner – from the very first update to the newest: Cubi, the green cube from the Cubiq logo, on a floating grass island next to something that fits the update. The picture of 0.3.0, for example, shows Cubi with the aurora wings, surrounded by his new companions. The scenes play quietly next to the title and load quickly, also on slower PCs.

Prefer things to stay still? With **Reduce animations** under **Settings → Appearance → Animations** you see the still picture of each scene instead. **Follow Windows/system** does the same when animations are turned off in Windows or on your desktop.

## The update card on the home page

On the **Home** page, below "Jump back in", the newest update waits for you as a card: its banner with the animated picture, the name of the update, the headlines of the notes as small chips, little previews of the screenshots and **Read the post**.

- Click the banner or **Read the post** to open the whole post – the same view as "What's new".
- Click a preview to open the post right at that screenshot. When an update has more pictures, a small badge like **+2** opens the rest.
- The **×** in the corner (**Hide until the next update**) hides the card. It comes back with the next update.

![The update card on the home page with the animated picture of the update, its headlines, screenshot previews and "Read the post"](/shots/launcher/update-card.webp)

The update also shows up in **News** on the home page. Under **All** you see only the newest launcher version between the other news; the filter **Launcher** lists every update and – with the Cubiq services on – the news of the Cubiq team.
