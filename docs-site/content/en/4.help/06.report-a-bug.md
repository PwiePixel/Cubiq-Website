Bugs and ideas go to the public **issue tracker** on the website:
[cubiq-launcher.invalid/issues](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/issues). Anyone can read it; to write,
vote or comment you sign in. The [roadmap](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/roadmap) shows what is planned, in
progress and done.

## Look first

Someone may have reported it already. Search the issue list – `#57` finds issue number 57. If you find your problem,
**vote it up** and add what's new in a comment instead of opening a second issue.

## Report on the website

::steps{level="3"}

### Sign in

Open [cubiq-launcher.invalid/issues](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/issues) and sign in with Microsoft,
or with the Cubiq Launcher.

### Open a new issue

Choose the type (**bug** or **feature**) and the area (**launcher**, **client** or **website**).

### Describe it

Give it a short, precise title (5 to 120 characters) and describe what happened. You can attach up to six images.

::

## Report from the game

For bugs in the Cubiq Client, report right from the game – the client can attach the technical details for you.

::steps{level="3"}

### Open the report page

Open the Cubiq menu with :kbd{value="Right Shift"} and choose **Report bug**.

### Write title and description

What did you do, what happened, what did you expect instead?

### Choose attachments

The Cubiq Client version, Minecraft version and loader, and your mod list are ticked by default. A log excerpt and a
screenshot are only sent if you tick them. **Take a new one** lets you press :kbd{value="F2"} for a fresh screenshot –
your text stays.

### Check the preview and send

The preview shows exactly what goes out and how many personal details were removed from the log. Press **Send now**.
You get the issue number and can open it in the browser.

::

::note
Reporting from the game needs the Cubiq services (turned on in the launcher under **Settings → Privacy**), the
**Cubiq Online Features** module in the Cubiq menu and a Microsoft account.
::

## What to include

A good report can be reproduced. Include:

- **Steps**: what you did, step by step, what happened, what you expected.
- **Versions**: Cubiq Launcher version, Cubiq Client version, Minecraft version and mod loader, Windows or Linux
  distribution.
- **The log**: use **Share log** in the launcher and paste the link (see [Logs](/en/help/logs)). For crashes, a crash
  report is best.
- **Mods**: your mod list, if mods are involved.
- **A screenshot or a short clip**, if you can see the problem.

::tip
Reports from the game already contain versions and mods. The log excerpt is cleaned on your PC first: tokens, UUIDs,
names, IP and e-mail addresses, user paths and chat lines are replaced.
::

## Who sees what

Title, description, images, versions and the mod list are public on the website, with your Minecraft name as author.
A log excerpt sent from the game is only visible to you and the Cubiq team.

## After reporting

You automatically follow issues you open or comment on. With the Cubiq services on, the launcher shows a notification
when the status changes, the team answers, a fix ships ("fixed in") or the issue is merged into another one – with a
**View** button that opens it in your browser.

## Not sure it's a bug?

Open an issue anyway and describe what you saw and what you expected – a question is welcome there too.
