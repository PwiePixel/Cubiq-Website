The Cubiq Launcher runs on your computer. It has **no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising**. It only
connects to a service when that is needed for something you asked it to do.

::note
This page is a short overview. The full privacy policy – for the launcher, the Cubiq services and the website – is on
the website: [cubiq-launcher.invalid/privacy](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/privacy). The launcher's part is also in
its source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/PwiePixel/Cubiq-launcher/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
::

## Who the launcher talks to

| Service | When |
|---|---|
| Microsoft, Xbox Live, Minecraft services | Signing in and starting the game |
| Mojang (game files, Java, version data, news) | Installing or starting a version; news on the start page |
| Mojang session server | Signing in to the Cubiq services, only after you agreed – the same check a Minecraft server does |
| Mojang profile services | Importing a skin by player name, showing player faces |
| The website of a link you enter | Only when you add a skin **By link** |
| Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge | Installing a mod loader |
| Modrinth | Browsing, installing or updating content; trending projects in the news |
| CurseForge | Only when you use CurseForge content |
| Minecraft servers in your server list | Showing player count and ping |
| GitHub | Launcher and Cubiq Client updates, release news; FFmpeg once when you turn on clips (Windows) |
| mclo.gs | Only when you press **Share log** and confirm |
| Discord app on your PC (local, no internet) | While **Show Discord status** is on |
| Cubiq server (through Cloudflare) | Only after you turned on the Cubiq services |

## What stays on your PC

- **Your Microsoft sign-in**: tokens are stored encrypted – on Windows with DPAPI, on Linux with a key from the system
  keyring. The launcher never sees your password.
- **Crash analysis**: the [crash helper](/en/help/crash-helper) works only on your PC.
- **Imports** from other launchers: their files are only read on your PC; their sign-in data is never read.
- **Clips and recordings** never leave your PC.
- **Play statistics**: your sessions are stored and counted only on your PC – friends can't see your
  [statistics](/en/launcher/statistics), and they aren't synced to your other PCs.

## Cubiq services

The Cubiq services are live: the launcher is connected to the Cubiq server. They bring Cubiq capes and cosmetics that
other Cubiq players see too, a [friends list with chat](/en/launcher/social), achievements and the sync of your own
skins, presets, theme, accent colour and language between your PCs. They are **off until you agree**.

### The question after signing in

As soon as you are signed in with a Minecraft account, the launcher asks you once: **Use Cubiq services?** The window
explains what you get and lists under **What's stored** exactly what the Cubiq server keeps:

- your Minecraft UUID and player name,
- your selected cape, redeemed codes and uploaded cape images,
- friends, requests and blocks,
- chat messages, pictures you send and reports – stored encrypted on the server until they are deleted; reported
  content is kept for moderation,
- screenshots you share as a link – public for anyone with the link, deleted automatically after 30 days,
- your privacy settings and timestamps (sign-in, creation),
- with sync on: your own skins, presets, theme, accent colour and language; the Cubiq Client syncs its modules, HUD
  layouts and keys the same way,
- your online status – kept in memory only, it expires after 3 minutes.

Click **Use Cubiq services** to turn them on, or **No, thanks** to keep them off – then the launcher sends nothing to the
Cubiq server. **Read the privacy policy** opens the part about the Cubiq services in your browser. Changed your mind later? Pages that need the services,
like **Social**, show **Learn more & turn on**, and the switch is under **Settings → Privacy**.

![The question "Use Cubiq services?" with the list of what is stored](/shots/help/cubiq-consent.webp)

### How signing in works

The launcher signs in with your Minecraft account the same way a Minecraft server checks a player. **The Cubiq server
never sees your password or your Minecraft access token.** It gives the launcher its own key, which is stored encrypted
on your PC and never handed to the game or to web pages. The Cubiq Client signs in by itself through the game session.

When you start Minecraft with the services switched on, the launcher tells the Cubiq Client which server to use. In the
game, the capes and cosmetics of other Cubiq players, the Cubiq badge next to their names, emotes, the Social menu with
chat, friends list and your friends' worlds, the sync of your modules and HUD profiles and bug reports from the Cubiq
menu all run over the same server as the launcher. With the services off, the Cubiq Client doesn't use them either.

### Where the Cubiq server runs

- The Cubiq server runs together with the Cubiq website at **cubiq-launcher.invalid**, on a rented server in Germany.
- **Cloudflare** sits in front of it as proxy and CDN: every request passes through Cloudflare, which sees your IP
  address and the request and filters attacks. Cloudflare may process this data outside the EU; it takes part in the
  EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
- **Mojang/Microsoft** confirm the sign-in.
- The server logs contain only technical data – **no IP addresses and no tokens**. Limits against abuse count requests
  per IP address and account in memory only.
- Your online status lives only in the server's memory and disappears 3 minutes after the last update.
- When you host a world for friends, a separate Cubiq relay server only forwards the game data and keeps nothing on disk.

Who runs the server and how to reach them is in the [imprint](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/imprint). Processors,
legal basis, retention periods and your rights under the GDPR are in the
[full privacy policy](https://cubiq-launcher.invalid/privacy).

::note
Builds without a Cubiq server – for example when you [build the launcher yourself](/en/developers/build-from-source#connect-your-own-cubiq-server)
– hide the Cubiq services and send nothing for them. Their pages then say "Online features aren't available".
::

## Switch off or delete: Settings → Privacy

Everything about the Cubiq services is under **Settings → Privacy**, in the block **Cubiq services**:

![Settings → Privacy with the Cubiq services switched on](/shots/help/privacy-cubiq-services.webp)

- **Use Cubiq services** – off means the launcher sends nothing to the Cubiq server and signs you out there. The Cubiq
  Client won't use the services in game either.
- **Sync with Cubiq account** – keeps your own skins, presets, theme, accent colour and language the same on every PC.
  Java and memory settings stay on each PC.
- **My applications** and **My sanctions** show your team applications with the team's answer and any sanctions of
  your account with reason, end and appeal. **My reports** under **Chat** shows the status of everything you reported.
- Per account: **Show Cubiq badge**, **Cape visible to others**, **Online status** (visible to **Friends** or
  **Nobody**), **Share server**, **Achievements visible to friends**, **Read receipts** and **Typing indicator**.
- **Website**: **Open website** opens the Cubiq website in your browser; **Sign in on the website** confirms a sign-in
  there with a code (see [Sign in](/en/getting-started/sign-in#sign-in-on-the-cubiq-website)).
- **Delete all Cubiq data …** and then **Yes, delete permanently** removes your Cubiq account with capes, uploads,
  codes, friends, chats (direct chats for both sides), online status and synced skins, presets and settings from the
  server right away. Afterwards the services are off. The skins and presets on your PC stay. Only active sanctions and
  reports about you are kept until they run out, so they can't be escaped by signing in again.

## Your settings at a glance

| Setting | Default | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cubiq services | off until you agree | Settings → Privacy |
| Sync with Cubiq account | on while the Cubiq services are on | Settings → Privacy |
| Online status visible to | Friends | Settings → Privacy |
| Allow sharing logs | on (uploads only when you confirm) | Settings → Privacy |
| Show Discord status | on | Settings → Privacy |
| Clips & recording | off | Settings → Clips & recording |
