## Minimap

The minimap is off by default. Turn it on in the Cubiq menu (:kbd{value="Right Shift"}) under **World → Minimap**; move and resize it in the HUD editor.

It shows real block colours (taken from the textures of your resource pack), biome-tinted grass, leaves and water, soft relief shading and water that gets darker with depth. Underground and in the Nether it switches to a **cave view** on its own, and under a roof it shows the **inside** of the building instead of the roof.

![The classic look: square and round minimap with compass letters, waypoint squares and a red arrow](/shots/client/minimap-classic.png)

### Classic or Cubiq look

On the minimap page, **Look** decides how the minimap and the world map look:

- **Classic (thin frame, compass letters, waypoint squares)** – the default. It looks like the classic minimap mods many players know: a thin grey frame with light from the top left, the compass letters N, E, S and W right on the edge of the map (north in red, each on a small dark plate, so they stay readable over snow and water), every waypoint as a small coloured square with its first letter – inside the map and at its edge – and a red arrow for you. Below the map your coordinates simply read `128, 64, -256`.
- **Cubiq (rounded frame, compass badges)** – a flat frame in the colours of your theme with a fine border, rounded corners on the square map and a soft shadow that keeps it easy to see over bright snow and dark caves alike. The compass directions sit on small round badges; north is filled with the accent colour, so you find it at once, even when the map turns with you. The dark, OLED and light colour schemes each get a matching frame, and the winter, fox and Halloween designs keep their own. Waypoints at the edge sit in flat, rounded boxes with a darker outline.

New players start with the square map. Prefer a round one? Switch **Shape** to **Round** – in the classic look it gets the same thin ring.

![The Cubiq look with compass badges and the view cone, on the left with the chunk grid](/shots/client/minimap-cubiq-look.png)

### View direction and chunk grid

- **View direction (cone in front of your arrow)** – a soft cone of light in front of your arrow shows where you are looking, on the minimap and on the world map. It fades out towards the front, so it never hides the map. It is on by default; switch it off if you don't need it.
- **Chunk grid** – thin lines every 16 blocks show the chunk borders, and every 128 blocks, where a new map region begins, the line is a little stronger. Handy for farms, spawn chunks and building along chunk borders. The grid turns with the map and hides by itself when you zoom out so far that the lines would crowd together. It is off by default.

## Fair Play

**Fair Play** turns off everything a map could use to cheat, for the minimap and the world map together:

- no cave view and no indoor view,
- players and mobs only show up when you could actually see them.

::steps{level="3"}
### Switch it on yourself
**Minimap → Fair Play (maps show only what you could see)**, or the Fair Play switch on the world map page.

### Or let the server decide
Servers that ask map mods for fair play (the common Xaero codes in chat or the server description) are respected automatically. A server can also turn the minimap off completely – the minimap page then tells you.
::

## Waypoints

::steps{level="3"}
### Create a waypoint
Press :kbd{value="B"}, pick a name and a colour, and save. It is created where you stand.

### Manage your waypoints
Press :kbd{value="N"} or click **Manage waypoints** on the **Waypoints** module page: edit, hide or delete.

### Find your way
In the world, each waypoint shows a **light beam** and its **distance**. After you die, a **death point** is set automatically.
::

Waypoints are saved per world or server on your PC. **Only up to (blocks, 0 = always)** hides far waypoints. The keys B and N are set on the module page (they are not in the Minecraft controls).

### Coordinates and waypoints from the chat

Coordinates that someone writes in the Minecraft chat (like `x: 100 y: 64 z: -20` or `100 64 -20`) are underlined – one click saves them as a waypoint. This happens only on your PC.

When someone shares a waypoint with one of the popular minimap mods – the chat line that starts with `xaero-waypoint:` – you see its name in its colour, its coordinates and its dimension instead, followed by **[+ Waypoint]** and **[Map]**:

- **[+ Waypoint]** opens the small window **Add shared waypoint** with name, colour and coordinates already filled in. Change what you like and click **Save** – the waypoint is on your map, in the right dimension too, for example a Nether waypoint while you are in the Overworld.
- **[Map]** opens the world map right on the spot and marks it. Right-click the marker and choose **Save as waypoint** to keep it, or **Remove marker**.
- :kbd{value="Shift"} + click puts the shared text into your chat line, so you can pass it on.

![A waypoint shared in the chat: "+ Waypoint" opens this window, already filled in](/shots/client/waypoint-from-chat.png)

This works in Minecraft 1.16 and newer. To switch it off together with the clickable coordinates: Cubiq menu → **Chat Improvements** → **Clickable coordinates and shared waypoints (save as waypoint)**.

### Share your own waypoints in the chat

On the world map, right-click a waypoint, a marked spot or any place and choose **Share in chat**. Your chat line opens with the waypoint written in that widely used format – press :kbd{value="Enter"} to send it. Players with the Cubiq Client get the **[+ Waypoint]** button, and players with those minimap mods can add it to their own map. Nothing is sent until you press Enter yourself.

## World map

Press :kbd{value="M"} to open the fullscreen world map. Everything you have explored stays on it – saved per world, server and dimension on your PC. If another map mod already uses M, the Cubiq key starts unbound instead of clashing.

In the classic look the world map uses the whole screen: at the top left you see the dimension and below it the coordinates under your mouse pointer, at the top right the buttons, at the bottom left the scale bar and at the bottom right the zoom. Waypoints are the same coloured squares as on the minimap, in the waypoint list too, and your arrow is red. In the Cubiq look the map has bars at the top and bottom; they, the waypoint list, the tooltips and the hints use the colours of your theme (dark, OLED or light), and your arrow uses the accent colour.

![The world map in the classic look: the right-click menu of a marked spot with "Save as waypoint", "Share in chat" and "Measure distance from here"](/shots/client/world-map-classic.png)

### Move around

- **Drag** or use the **arrow keys** to move; after dragging, the map glides on for a moment.
- The **mouse wheel** zooms smoothly towards the mouse pointer; :kbd{value="+"} and :kbd{value="−"} zoom in and out.
- :kbd{value="Home"} or **Center on me** takes you back to your own position.
- The **level button** switches between surface, cave levels and indoor level.

### Right-click menu

Right-click a free spot for **Create waypoint here**, a waypoint for **Edit**, **Hide** (**Show** for a hidden one) and **Delete**, or a marked spot for **Save as waypoint** and **Remove marker**. Also in the menu: **Share with friends**, **Share in chat**, **Measure distance from here**, **Center here** and **Follow me**.

### Measure distances

1. Right-click the starting point and choose **Measure distance from here**.
2. A dashed line follows your mouse and shows the distance in metres, from 1,000 blocks in kilometres. A click sets the end point. The measurement stays while you move and zoom the map.
3. To remove it, press :kbd{value="Esc"} or right-click → **Clear measurement**. Esc removes the measurement first, before it closes the map.

The bottom left corner shows a **scale bar** with a round length – 20 m, 100 m or 1 km, for example – that adapts while you zoom, so you can judge distances at a glance.

![Measuring a distance on the world map in the Cubiq look, with the scale bar at the bottom left](/shots/client/world-map-measure.png)

### Jump to coordinates

Type coordinates into the search of the waypoint list (**Search or type X Z …**), for example `120 -40`, `120 64 -40` or `x 120 z -40`. A row **Go to 120 / -40** appears above the list; click it or press :kbd{value="Enter"}, and the map centres on that spot and marks it. Right-click the marker → **Save as waypoint** keeps it. Handy for coordinates from the chat, from a friend or from a seed map.

![Coordinates typed into the search: the map jumps there and marks the spot (Cubiq look, light colour scheme)](/shots/client/world-map-go-to.png)

### Waypoint list

The pin button opens a list of all waypoints of this world: search by name or coordinate, filter by dimension, see the distance (Nether and Overworld converted ×8) and turn each one on or off. Click a waypoint to center the map on it, the pencil edits it, **+** adds one in the middle of the map.

### Other dimensions

Click the dimension name at the top to look at the Nether, the End or other dimensions of this world you have mapped – without going there. In the Nether and the Overworld the coordinates also show the matching position on the other side (×8 or ÷8), handy for portals.

### Export as image

The image button saves **Whole explored map** or **Visible area only** as a PNG in your `screenshots` folder. Unexplored parts stay transparent, very large maps are scaled down to the export size (default 8192 pixels). The export runs in the background and a chat message tells you the file name.

### World map settings

Show waypoints, players, hostile mobs and animals, chunk grid when zoomed in, smooth zoom, keep gliding after dragging, view other dimensions, matching Nether/Overworld coordinates, export size and **Map storage on disk** (default 256 MB – the oldest areas are removed first). The look of the world map follows **Look** on the minimap page.

## Share waypoints with friends

With the Cubiq online features you can send a waypoint – or where you stand – to a friend or group in the [social chat](/en/client/social-in-game). It arrives as a card with name, coordinates, dimension and server. Your friend can **Save** it as their own waypoint (on the same server or in the same world) or **Show** it: the world map opens with a marker on the spot, which they can keep with a right-click → **Save as waypoint**.

## Version notes

::note
The minimap and the world map work in every version from 1.7.10 to 26.3. On 1.7.10 and 1.13.2 there is no **Waypoints** module – create and edit waypoints on the world map with a right-click; the death point is set there too. Waypoints shared in the game chat (**[+ Waypoint]**, **[Map]**) need Minecraft 1.16 or newer. Sharing waypoints with friends needs the Cubiq online features and is not available on 1.7.10 and 1.13.2.
::
