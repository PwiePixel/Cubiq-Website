With the Cubiq Client, the cosmetics you pick in the launcher come to life in the game: wings on your back, a companion that flies around your head and one that walks along next to you. Everyone who plays with the Cubiq Client sees them – and your own companion on the ground reacts to you: pet it or feed it with a right-click.

## What you see in the game

- **Ground companions** follow you wherever you go. They walk after you, jump up blocks and swim through water. When you run fast, they slide after you to keep up – the penguin on its belly, the snowman like a curling stone. If you get more than 20 blocks ahead or your companion gets stuck, it simply pops up next to you again. You see your own companion in first person too.
- **Look at a companion** from close by for a moment and it puts its hands up.
- **The fox kit** now and then pounces on a berry while it stands still and runs off with it – past you and back again.
- **Wings** move with you – on the ground, in the air, where they fan out wide, and while you sneak. While you wear an elytra, they are hidden.
- **Companions at your head** – the shooting star and Mini-Cubi – fly around your head.
- **At night** many of them glow: Cubi's eyes and leaves, the axolotl's gills, the snowman's eyes, the firefly's lantern, the aurora wings, the shooting star and Mini-Cubi's face and sprout.

Players without the Cubiq Client don't see your cosmetics. Which cosmetics there are and how they look in the launcher preview: [Skins, capes & cosmetics](/en/launcher/wardrobe#cosmetics).

## Choose them in the game

Your cosmetics are the same in the launcher and in the game. To change them while you play, open the [wardrobe](/en/client/emotes-and-capes#wardrobe) and go to the **Cosmetics** tab.

::steps{level="3"}
### Pick a section
**Companion**, **Wings** and **Companion (ground)** each start with **Nothing**, followed by your cosmetics. Cosmetics you haven't unlocked yet are listed under **Locked**, with a hint on how to get them.

### Try it on
Click a card to see it in the preview – while you try it on, only you see it. The sun and moon button switches the preview between day and night, so you can check what glows.

### Put it on
Click **Put on** – now everyone with the Cubiq Client sees it. **Take off** removes it again.
::

::note
The cards show the German names of the cosmetics – for example **Watschelpinguin** for the penguin and **Füchschen** for the fox kit. The [list of free cosmetics](/en/launcher/wardrobe#nine-free-cosmetics) shows every name side by side.
::

## Pet and feed your companion

Point at your own companion on the ground and right-click:

| In your hand | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Nothing | You **pet** it: it leans into your hand, closes its eyes happily, wiggles, and little hearts rise above it. |
| Something to eat – bread, an apple, a cookie … | You **feed** it: it looks up, takes three bites with crumbs flying and chews happily. |
| Anything else – a block, a sword, a bow … | Minecraft as usual – your companion is left alone. |

While it enjoys the moment, your companion stands still and looks at you. Each reaction plays to the end; after a short pause you can start the next one. It works with all six ground companions: Cubi, the penguin, the fox kit, the axolotl, the snowman and the firefly.

![The penguin being fed: three bites, crumbs flying](/shots/client/companion-feeding.png)

### Good to know

- **Nothing is used up.** Feeding is just for fun: your food stays in your inventory – even if your companion doesn't react right then.
- **The main hand counts.** With an empty main hand and food in your off-hand you feed your companion; a torch or shield in your off-hand doesn't stop you from petting it.
- **Only your own companion.** The right-click only goes to your companion when you point right at it within normal reach. Other players' companions don't react.
- **Plants don't get in the way.** Grass, flowers, a field of wheat or a thin layer of snow in front of your companion don't block the click. Blocks, doors, chests, levers and villagers in front of it work as always.
- **Sneak to use what's behind it.** If your companion stands right in front of something you want to use, sneak while you right-click – the game then ignores your companion.
- **Only you see it.** The reaction only plays on your screen.
- **Calm and close.** Your companion reacts when it stands calmly on the ground next to you – not while it swims, slides after you or runs off with a berry. If you walk away, it stops and follows you.

::tip
Want to see the reactions without starting the game? The companion preview in the launcher has **Pet** and **Feed** buttons too – see [Pet and feed in the preview](/en/launcher/wardrobe#pet-and-feed-in-the-preview).
::

## Settings

Both settings belong to the module **Cubiq Online Features** in the Cubiq menu (:kbd{value="Right Shift"}). Wings and companions need the [Cubiq online features](/en/client/overview#cubiq-online-features).

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show Cubiq cosmetics | on | Shows wings and companions – yours and those of other players. Switched off, you see none of them and can't pet your companion. |
| Pet and feed your companions (right-click) | on | Switched off, the right-click works exactly as before. |

## Version notes

::note
- Wings and all companions are shown on Forge 1.8.9 – 1.12.2 and on 1.15.2 and newer (Fabric, Forge and NeoForge).
- On Fabric 1.14.4 you see the companions at the head (shooting star and Mini-Cubi), but no wings and no companions on the ground.
- On 1.7.10, 1.13.2 and Forge 1.14.4 they are not shown.
::
